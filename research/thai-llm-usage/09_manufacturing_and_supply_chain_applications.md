# Thai LLM Market Research — Iteration 09

## Focus: Manufacturing and Supply Chain Applications

## Research Highlights
- Thailand's LLM opportunity is strongest where Thai-language understanding directly impacts revenue or service quality.
- Adoption momentum is uneven: large enterprises move via pilots, while mid-market buyers require packaged ROI stories.
- Procurement decisions increasingly combine model quality, governance readiness, and local implementation capability.

## Market Analysis
- **Customer Segments:**
  - Tier-1 enterprises (banks, telecoms, retailers) seek integrated copilots and automated customer operations.
  - Government and education buyers prioritize sovereign data controls, affordability, and Thai language inclusiveness.
  - SMEs need pre-configured templates and low-change deployment paths to reduce implementation risk.
- **Buying Criteria:**
  - Accuracy on Thai prompts, code-switched inputs, and domain terminology.
  - Secure deployment architecture (private cloud, role-based access, audit logs).
  - Total cost predictability under variable token usage.
- **Competitive Signals:**
  - General-purpose global LLMs lead in ecosystem maturity.
  - Regional players compete via localization speed and partnership-led deployment.
  - Integrators influence final vendor choice through procurement and implementation confidence.

## Ongoing Bulletpoint Research Track
- Demand drivers: customer experience automation, multilingual service, and labor productivity.
- Supply constraints: GPU availability, cost-to-serve, and limited Thai high-quality datasets.
- Trust factors: data residency, PDPA compliance, explainability, and model safety controls.
- Commercial levers: value-based pricing, workflow integration depth, and measurable ROI.
- Execution priorities: start narrow, benchmark frequently, and scale through reusable platforms.
